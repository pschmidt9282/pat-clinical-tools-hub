Sulfonylurea Safety & Prescribing Warning:
Sulfonylureas (Glipizide, Glimepiride, Glyburide) are DISFAVORED as first- or second-line therapy in modern ADA guidelines. They cause significant hypoglycemia, weight gain, accelerate beta-cell exhaustion, and lack cardiorenal protective benefits. Prefer SGLT2i, GLP-1 RA, Tirzepatide, or DPP-4i.
