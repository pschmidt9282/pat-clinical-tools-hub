3. Non-Pharmacologic Gold Standard: CBT-I Protocols

First-Line Guideline Recommendation: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) is recommended as initial treatment for ALL adult insomnia patients prior to or alongside pharmacotherapy.
• Stimulus Control (20-Minute Rule): If unable to sleep after ~20 minutes, get out of bed immediately. Go to another dimly lit room and do a quiet activity (e.g. read paper book). Return to bed ONLY when drowsy. Bed is strictly for sleep & sex.
• Sleep Restriction Therapy: Match time spent in bed to actual total sleep time (minimum 5.5 hours) to consolidate sleep drive. Gradually expand time in bed by 15 mins once sleep efficiency > 85%.
• Sleep Hygiene Rules: Set a fixed wake-up time 7 days/week (no sleeping in on weekends). Avoid blue light screens 60 mins before bed. Keep bedroom cool (65–68°F) and pitch dark.
