Diagnostic Work Up Sequence

Step 1
Presentation & Labs
Acute, severe, unilateral flank pain radiating to groin, hematuria, nausea.
  • Urinalysis: Microhematuria, check pH, rule out UTI.
  • BMP: Cr (renal function), Calcium.
Step 2
Diagnostic Imaging
Low-dose non-contrast CT Abdomen/Pelvis (CT KUB): Gold standard (sens. 95-97%).

*Use Renal Ultrasound for pregnant patients, children, or serial monitoring.
Step 3
Stone Size Triage
Determine treatment pathway:
  • <5 mm: 90% pass. Hydration + pain control + MET.
  • 5-10 mm: 50% pass. Consider Tamsulosin (MET).
  • >10 mm: Unlikely to pass. Refer to Urology for lithotripsy/ureteroscopy.
Step 4
Red Flags (ER/Urology)
Immediate Urology intervention if:
  • Obstructing stone + infection (fever, pyuria). Urologic emergency!
  • AKI, anuria (e.g. solitary kidney).
  • Intractable pain/vomiting.
