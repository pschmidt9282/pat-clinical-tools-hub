ADHD Pharmacotherapy Decision Matrix

Interactive pharmacological guide for the management of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Compare first-line stimulants (methylphenidate vs. amphetamine formulations), second-line non-stimulant SNRIs, and third-line alpha-2 agonists. Tailor selections based on comorbidity risk, duration of coverage, and cost.

Clinical Selection Guidance (AACE/AACAP/APSARD guidelines): Stimulants are first-line for moderate-to-severe ADHD. Cardiorespiratory risk must be evaluated prior to initiation. Non-stimulants (Atomoxetine, Viloxazine) or alpha-2 agonists are preferred if stimulants are contraindicated, poorly tolerated (severe anxiety, tics, insomnia), or when there is active substance use disorder.
