5. Non-Pharmacologic & Risk Reduction Protocol

• Smoking Cessation (Single Most Effective Intervention): Offer Varenicline, Bupropion SR, or Nicotine Replacement (Patch + Gum/Lozenge) alongside behavioral counseling at every visit.
• Pulmonary Rehabilitation: Indicated for all patients in GOLD Groups B and E. Improves exercise capacity, dyspnea, and health-related quality of life.
• Vaccinations (Essential): Influenza (annual), COVID-19, Pneumococcal (PCV20 or PCV15 followed by PPSV23), RSV (single dose for ≥60 yo), Tdap.
• Long-Term Oxygen Therapy (LTOT): Indicated if resting PaO2 ≤ 55 mmHg (or SaO2 ≤ 88%), or PaO2 56–59 mmHg with cor pulmonale or polycythemia (hematocrit > 55%). Titrate target SaO2 88–92%.
